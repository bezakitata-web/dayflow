<h1 align="center">Day Flow</h1>

<p align="center">
  <b>A minimal, offline-first Android habit tracker and daily planner designed for fast goal setting, streak tracking, and customizable routines.</b>
</p>

---

### ✨ Features
* **100% Offline & Private:** Built with a local-first architecture. Your data, habits, and daily schedules never leave your device.
* **Habit & Streak Tracking:** Build positive routines and monitor your daily consistency effortlessly.
* **Fast Daily Planning:** Organize your day in seconds with a clean, distraction-free interface.
